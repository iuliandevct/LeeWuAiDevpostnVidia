# Sea-fastening reference

MetaCAD source checked 2026-08-01:
https://metacad.io/en/commercial/sea-fastening-survey/

## MetaCAD-described scope

The page describes local browser calculations for CSS Code Annex 13 accelerations/force balance, MSL/CS arithmetic for portable lashings, friction-aware sliding/balance checks, a pre-departure checklist and PDF working report.

It explicitly excludes replacement of the appointed MWS, physical witness, NDT supervision, weather-routing review, final Certificate of Approval and detailed engineering of welded stoppers, deck strengthening and project-specific pad-eyes.

## Common governing references

- IMO CSS Code and relevant Cargo Securing Manual guidance, including the current applicable Annex 13 basis.
- SOLAS Chapter VI requirements for stowage and securing.
- DNV-ST-N001 when invoked by the project, insurer or MWS.
- IMCA M 162 where applicable as practical industry guidance.
- Vessel CSM, class/flag rules, project specifications, underwriter warranty clauses and MWS instructions.

Do not assume the MetaCAD page's listed circular/revision is the latest applicable edition; verify it from the official publisher.

## Engineering boundaries

- Portable lashing capacity is not welded-fastening capacity.
- MSL/CS or breaking-strength arithmetic does not validate pad-eyes, D-rings, weld groups, braces, stoppers, foundations or supporting deck structure.
- Apply loads along actual restraint geometry and account for unequal stiffness/load sharing where required.
- Validate friction assumptions for the real interface, contamination, weather and compression.
- Confirm cargo-frame and OEM allowable loads; a strong sea fastening can still damage the cargo.
- Check concentrated bearing, load spreading, local plating/stiffeners/girders and global hull strength.
- Consider installation tolerances, fatigue/cyclic loading, pretension loss and accessibility for inspection/re-tightening.

## Survey evidence

Expect project-specific drawings/calculations, material traceability, equipment certificates, weld procedures and welder qualifications, NDT records, structural approval where required, as-built measurements, installation photos, final lashing/tension records, weather/voyage review, punch-list close-out and signed stakeholder acceptance according to the warranty.

Insurance consequences depend on the actual policy and warranty wording. Do not make categorical coverage statements without reviewing those documents.
