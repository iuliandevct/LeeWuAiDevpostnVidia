8. CARGO OPERATION

8.1. Cargo Operation Description

The 'TRANSFORMER BODY' cargo units were loaded from stevedores open concrete platform adjacent to berth 120 by harbor mobile crane and rigging equipment provided by the stevedores.

The wooden cases and 'TRANSFORMER OIL TANK' cargo units were conveyed to the quayside alongside berth 119 by trailers and safely loaded onboard the on-carrying river barge.

8.2. Cargo Lifting Gear

Stevedore's lifting appliances:
  • Harbor mobile crane SWL104[t] range 5-42[m] used for light packages
  • Harbor mobile crane SWL125[t] range 5-53.5[m] used for transformer body

8.3. Underhook Rigging

Underhook rigging equipment supplied by stevedores, consist of:

TRANSFORMER BODY:
  • 4xWLL30[t] length 10[m] polyester round slings
TRANSFORMER OIL TANKS
  • 4xWLL5[t] length 7.2[m] polyester flat slings
  • 4xWLL8[t] connection shackles
Wooden cases
  • 4xWLL5[t] length 7.2[m] polyester flat slings (folded in double)
  • 4xWLL8[t] connection shackles
  • 4xWLL5[t] length 7.2[m] polyester flat slings
  • 2xWLL6[t] lifting hooks

Pre-lift cursory visual inspection to underhook rigging equipment and configuration was conducted with satisfactory results.

8.4. Lifting Operation

The lifting operation was smooth with limited lifting, luffing and slewing speed; no rough handling or uneven lifting resulting in cargo tilt was noted throughout lifting operations.

The following exception was noted:

Exception:
Wooden case side planks locally broken during handling by forklift (overturned to one side) – package no. 35/93 – additional inspection is recommended at the receiver premises, upon wooden case opening
